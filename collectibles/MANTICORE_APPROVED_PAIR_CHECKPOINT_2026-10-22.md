## Standard effects
Interactive touch/mouse tilt, original gold frame and frame-only metallic gold shine. No holographic background effects.

## Holographic effects
Interactive tilt, gold trim shine, rainbow holographic foil, curved tilt-responsive reflection, rising star-shaped sparkles and automatic passing shine. Foil, wave, chroma and sparkles are masked away from the black Manticore; automatic passing shine alone may cross the silhouette, following the approved Chimera/Basilisk style.
